# Website Content Draft: The Green Heritage Resort and Banquet

**Status:** Draft copy. Confirm the business details and services before publication.  
**Writing approach:** Plain, specific, human-sounding language based on the supplied Human Writing Guidelines. Avoid invented awards, testimonials, superlatives, unsupported amenity claims and generic promotional filler.

## Brand and content direction

The name suggests a green, resort-style setting, but the website should use actual property photography to establish the look and feel. The design should feel welcoming and suitable for both stays and events, if the owner confirms both are active services.

Suggested visual direction: deep forest green, warm ivory, muted leaf green and restrained brass accents. Use an elegant serif for main headings and a clean sans serif for body copy. Avoid excessive gradients, decorative badges and unnecessary animation.
