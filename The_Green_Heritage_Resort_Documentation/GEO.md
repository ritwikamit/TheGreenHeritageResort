# GEO: Generative Engine Optimisation

Generative engine optimisation aims to make the resort's information easier for answer systems to find and interpret. No method guarantees inclusion or citation.

## Source of truth
Keep the official website as the clearest source for business name, location, contact details, services and social links. Keep legitimate directory listings consistent with it.

## Content
Publish concise, specific facts, useful FAQs, original property photos, directions and current policies. Use headings and internal links that make the information easy to understand.

## Local signals
Maintain an accurate Google Business Profile, consistent business details, genuine customer reviews and legitimate local/event references. Do not purchase spam links or fabricate awards and reviews.

## Technical discoverability
Keep public pages crawlable, maintain a current sitemap, use descriptive page titles and ensure key information is available in accessible page content.

## Avoid
Mass-generated thin pages, hidden keywords, fake citations and promises of placement in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini or other systems.
