# Owner Questionnaire

## Identity and contact
1. What is the exact official business name?
2. Is `thegreenheritageresort.in` the current official domain?
3. Confirm the exact address and Google Maps pin.
4. Which phone number should be public? Are calls and WhatsApp active on it?
5. What is the official enquiry email?
6. What are the actual customer-facing hours?

## Services and facilities
7. Are rooms available? Provide room types, current tariff and check-in/check-out rules.
8. Are weddings and other events hosted? Which event types?
9. What are the capacities of each indoor/outdoor space?
10. Is the swimming pool available to all guests? What are its hours/rules?
11. Confirm parking, Wi-Fi, breakfast and other amenities.
12. What are the catering, decoration, outside-vendor and event timing policies?
13. Are accessibility facilities available?

## Booking and content
14. What deposit, payment, cancellation and refund terms apply?
15. Which phone/email should receive website enquiries?
16. Can the website use photos/videos from social media, or will original assets be supplied?
17. Are customer testimonials approved for use?
18. Who owns the domain, hosting, Google Business Profile and social accounts?
19. Who will update prices, photos, facilities and policies?
