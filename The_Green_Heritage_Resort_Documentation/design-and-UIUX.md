# Design and UI/UX Direction

## Creative direction
Build the visual identity around the property's real architecture, greenery, rooms and event spaces. Avoid generic stock resort imagery that could misrepresent the location.

## Suggested palette
Use the official logo and owner-approved photos to refine the palette. A sensible starting point is forest green, warm ivory, muted leaf green and a restrained brass accent. Confirm with the owner before treating these as official brand colours.

## Typography
Use an elegant, readable serif for main headings and a clean sans serif for body copy. Avoid decorative fonts for paragraphs and avoid typewriter styles.

## Homepage layout
1. Header with logo, key navigation and enquiry action.
2. Hero with a real property image, concise headline and contact CTA.
3. Short introduction and location.
4. Stay section, only if accommodation is confirmed.
5. Weddings/events section, only if event services are confirmed.
6. Verified facilities.
7. Gallery of approved photos.
8. Location/directions.
9. FAQs.
10. Contact form and footer.

## Interaction
Use subtle transitions. Avoid intrusive popups, constant motion, excessive floating badges and autoplay audio. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Mobile experience
Ensure readable type, tap-friendly buttons, compressed images, simple forms, accessible navigation and easy access to directions/contact details.
