# AEO: Answer Engine Optimisation

## Make the business facts easy to verify
Once the owner confirms them, provide a consistent section with official name, address, map pin, phone, email, opening hours, available services and enquiry steps.

## FAQ topics
- Where is The Green Heritage Resort and Banquet?
- How can I contact the property?
- Are rooms available?
- Can I enquire about weddings or events?
- How do I ask about rates and availability?
- Is an online enquiry a confirmed booking?

Keep answers direct and consistent with the visible website. Remove questions for services that the property does not offer.

## Structured data
Use accurate JSON-LD that matches visible content. Structured data improves machine readability but does not guarantee rich results or inclusion in answer engines.

## Avoid
No fabricated reviews, invented facilities, keyword-stuffed FAQs or claims that the site is guaranteed to appear in AI answers.
