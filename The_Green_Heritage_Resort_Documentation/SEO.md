# SEO and Local Search Plan

## Primary intent themes
Validate these against Search Console and keyword tools:
- The Green Heritage Resort and Banquet
- resort in Muzaffarpur
- banquet hall in Muzaffarpur
- wedding venue in Muzaffarpur
- resort near Bakarpur
- resort Gobarsahi Sakri Road
- The Green Heritage Resort contact / directions
- rooms or event venue in Muzaffarpur, only if these services are confirmed

Do not treat this list as evidence of search volume.

## Site structure
Create dedicated pages only when each has useful, distinct content. Avoid doorway pages that simply repeat the same copy with different keywords.

## On-page SEO
- Unique title and meta description per page.
- One clear H1 per page and descriptive subheadings.
- Canonical URLs and Open Graph metadata.
- Descriptive alt text for genuine property images.
- Internal links between relevant pages.
- Consistent official name, address, phone and opening hours.

## Technical SEO
- HTTPS and canonical-domain redirects.
- XML sitemap and correct robots.txt.
- Correct 404 responses.
- No indexing of preview deployments, admin routes or private endpoints.
- Mobile-friendly layouts and optimised images.
- Monitor Search Console indexing and Core Web Vitals.

## Local SEO
Verify and maintain the correct Google Business Profile. Keep the name, address, map pin, primary phone, hours, category and website consistent across legitimate listings. Upload owner-approved photos and respond to reviews professionally. Do not buy reviews or publish fabricated testimonials.

## Structured data
Use the most appropriate LocalBusiness/Hotel/Resort-related schema only when its fields are verified and match visible page content. Do not invent ratings, review snippets, opening hours, prices or amenities.

## Measurement
Track organic impressions, clicks, search queries, directions, calls and enquiry submissions. Never send names, phone numbers or message text to analytics.
